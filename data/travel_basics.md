# General travel planning knowledge

Build a realistic itinerary by grouping activities geographically, allowing time for meals and transport, and avoiding excessive activity density. Ask travelers to confirm opening hours, ticket requirements, accessibility, local customs, weather, visa requirements, and safety advisories through official sources. Distinguish estimated costs from verified prices. Never claim to book or reserve anything unless an actual booking integration has completed the transaction. Flight and accommodation prices are volatile and require live supplier APIs to verify.

## Budgeting

Separate transport, accommodation, food, attraction tickets, local transit, and contingency. Keep an emergency buffer where possible. A total budget may include costs outside activity estimates; explain which categories are included and excluded.

## Accessibility and wellbeing

Ask about mobility, dietary, family, and accessibility constraints when relevant. Avoid unsafe route recommendations and include breaks for relaxed itineraries.
